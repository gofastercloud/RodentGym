# Code of conduct

RodentGym is a small, friendly project, and we want it to stay a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or contribute without worrying about how they'll be treated.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, to macOS development, or to English.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback on your own work graciously.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, sexualised content, trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, and anything else a reasonable person would consider unwelcoming.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you're representing the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that isn't right, please tell the maintainer privately through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/gofastercloud), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the project when that's needed to keep it welcoming.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
